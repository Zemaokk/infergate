# Collaboration agreement

The detailed [author/AI learning agreement](archive/2026-10-08/docs/COLLABORATION_CONTRACT.md)
is preserved in the archive. Its work boundaries and learning rules remain the
operative agreement; dated milestone updates describe history. Read it before
changing core mechanisms.

- Core mechanisms (A): author first draft, focused review, verification, teach-back.
- Framework/lifecycle integration (B): author key path, joint review of boundaries.
- Engineering support (C): AI may lead fixtures, tooling, environments, packaging, and documentation within authorized scope.
- Scope and final design interpretation remain with the author. Contribution
  percentages in the original agreement are task-level targets, not measured results.
- A substantial completed task includes a concise English Conventional Commit
  suggestion; committing and pushing require explicit authorization.

Current [development context](explanation/development.md) and
[contribution guidance](../CONTRIBUTING.md) supplement the detailed agreement.
Current acceptance status is in [Validation](reference/validation.md).
